Accessibility statement

Central Pines Regional Council wants everyone to be able to use our data and tools, including people who use assistive technology.

Our standard

We aim for every page on this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, following the North Carolina Department of Information Technology's Web Content Accessibility guidance.

What we do

  • We test pages with automated accessibility checkers before publishing them.
  • We check that every page works with a keyboard alone and shows where keyboard focus is.
  • We check color contrast, and we don't use color as the only way to convey information.
  • We check that pages reflow on small screens and at high zoom without losing content.
  • We give charts and maps text summaries, and data tables or downloads where practical.

Known limitations

This site is new (Beta), and some interactive maps and complex charts may not yet be fully accessible to every user. If any content is hard for you to use, we'll gladly provide the information in another format, such as a data table, spreadsheet or written summary.

Contact us

If you run into an accessibility barrier on this site or need information in another format, please tell us. Include the page address and a short description of the problem.
